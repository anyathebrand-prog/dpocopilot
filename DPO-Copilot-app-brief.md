# DPO Copilot — App Brief (v0.1)

## One-liner
A compliance workspace for Nigerian DPOs and DPCOs that turns NDP Act 2023 obligations into structured workflows, with an AI copilot that drafts, checks, and reminds inside each one.

## The Problem
Most DPOs run compliance on Word templates, Excel registers, and WhatsApp follow-ups. Every new client means rebuilding the same data map, RoPA, DPIA, and policies from scratch. Deadlines like the annual Compliance Audit Returns (CAR) and the 72-hour breach notification window are tracked manually. Generic AI tools can draft text, but they don't know the client's actual data, the evidence trail, or Nigerian regulatory specifics.

## The Solution
One platform where each obligation is a guided workflow with structured data underneath. The copilot works on that data, not a blank chat box. It drafts a DPIA from the processing activities already mapped, flags gaps before audit season, and generates a breach notice from the incident log.

## Target Users
- **Independent DPOs:** managing several client organisations at once.
- **DPCOs (licensed compliance firms):** serving many clients; need team seats and a multi-client dashboard.
- **In-house compliance leads:** at SMEs, banks, fintechs, schools, and hospitals.

## Core Modules (MVP)
- **Client onboarding & data mapping:** a questionnaire that builds the data inventory and flags whether the org is likely a data controller/processor of major importance.
- **RoPA builder:** a living record of processing activities that feeds every other module.
- **DPIA workflow:** guided risk assessment; the AI drafts findings and mitigations for DPO review.
- **Policy generator:** privacy notices, retention policies, and consent forms, pre-filled from the client's data map.
- **Breach management:** incident log with a 72-hour countdown, severity scoring, and draft NDPC and data-subject notifications.
- **DSAR tracker:** intake, deadlines, and response templates for data subject requests.
- **Audit & CAR prep:** evidence vault plus a readiness score before filing.
- **Compliance calendar:** deadlines, renewals, and training reminders across all clients.

## Where the AI Sits
- **Drafting:** policies, DPIAs, notices, and reports.
- **Gap analysis:** e.g. "This client processes health data but has no DPIA."
- **Regulatory Q&A:** grounded in the NDP Act 2023 and NDPC guidance.
- **Document summarisation:** reads client documents during onboarding.

The DPO always reviews and approves before anything goes out. Human-in-the-loop keeps outputs defensible.

## Why It Wins
- **Nigeria-first:** global tools like OneTrust are expensive and GDPR-shaped.
- **Multi-client by design:** matches how DPOs and DPCOs actually work.
- **Practitioner-built:** templates and workflows come from real DPO experience.

## Business Model
Per-seat SaaS with three tiers:
- **Solo DPO**
- **DPCO Firm:** multi-client plus team seats
- **Enterprise**

Optional add-on: per-audit / CAR filing package.
